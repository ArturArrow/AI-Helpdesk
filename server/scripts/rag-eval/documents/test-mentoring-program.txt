Przyszly Programista -- Mentoring Program

What it is

Mentoring is a 1:1 video call with one of our mentors (senior developers who work with the course authors). You can use it for code review, career advice or help with a project.

Who can book

Mentoring is available only to people who bought the Pro plan or a course bundle. Buyers of a single course can't book sessions; they can upgrade to Pro in the dashboard and pay only the difference.

Price and limits

A 60-minute session costs 249 zł. Pro plan members get 2 sessions per month included and can buy more at 199 zł each. You can book at most 4 sessions per calendar month in total.

Booking and cancelling

Book sessions from the calendar in Dashboard > Mentoring. A session can be cancelled or moved free of charge up to 24 hours before it starts. A session cancelled later, or missed, is charged in full and doesn't come back to the monthly allowance.

Before the session

Send the code you want reviewed (a GitHub link is best) at least 48 hours before the session, so the mentor can prepare. Sessions are held on Google Meet; the link is in the booking confirmation email. Sessions are in Polish or English.
